Name: YouTubeForge — YouTube Transcript & SRT
Short (≤132): YouTube transcript in one click: searchable captions, SRT/VTT, open on forgeyt.com. Free, no account.
Category: Productivity

Detailed description:
YouTubeForge grabs public captions from the YouTube watch page you are on and opens a searchable transcript on forgeyt.com — free, no account.

What you can do after:
• Search lines and jump timestamps
• Copy text or Markdown
• Download SRT / VTT subtitle files
• Translate captions or save audio (MP3) on the same result page

How it works:
1. Open any public YouTube video that has captions (human or auto).
2. Click “Get transcript” on the page or the toolbar icon.
3. Captions are read in your browser, sent to YouTubeForge, and the result opens at forgeyt.com/?v=…

Single purpose: On a YouTube watch page, fetch public captions in this browser and open the transcript on YouTubeForge.

Privacy: https://forgeyt.com/privacy
Support: https://forgeyt.com/extension
Homepage: https://forgeyt.com/extension

Permission justification:
- activeTab / youtube.com: read the video id and fetch public captions the tab can already see
- forgeyt.com: POST /api/ingest then open /?v=

Do not put AdSense in the extension. Ads are only on the website.
